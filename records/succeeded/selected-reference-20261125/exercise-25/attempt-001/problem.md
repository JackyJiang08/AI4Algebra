## 6. Exercise 25 — A condition forcing commutativity · Moderate

Prove that if $x^2=1$ for every $x\in G$, then $G$ is abelian.

**Value:** Trains you to extract a global structural conclusion from an identity
satisfied by every element.

Lean mapping: The conclusion expresses commutativity for every pair in the given group. The hypothesis applies to every element, including the identity.
