# Exercise 27 — Constructing a cyclic subgroup

Task `exercise-27`. Split `train`. Source `data/training_questions.md` (SHA-256 `2619c9aeadc349114f8ebd39d67dfa7eb4ae268d82e6548626c54f47f26b5084`).

Let $x\in G$. Prove that

$$
\langle x\rangle=\{x^n\mid n\in\mathbb{Z}\}
$$

is a subgroup of $G$, called the cyclic subgroup generated by $x$.

**Value:** Applies the subgroup criterion and connects integer powers to group structure.

Lean mapping: The subgroup carrier is exactly the set of all integer powers of x. The statement leaves the subgroup witness to the proof.
